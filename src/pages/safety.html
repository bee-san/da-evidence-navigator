Stay safe when you use this website

If you need to hide what you are doing on this website, or protect yourself from someone, it can be safer not to use your home computer or your own mobile phone. For example, use a computer at a library, or borrow a phone from someone you trust.

What this website saves

This website does not use cookies and does not save anything you enter. Everything you type stays on the page and is cleared when you leave it.

Your browser still saves a record of the pages you have looked at. This is called your browsing history.

How to delete your browsing history

Be careful: clearing all your history could make someone more suspicious. Try to only remove the websites you want to keep private.

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Samsung Internet

How the ‘Exit this page’ button works

The ‘Exit this page’ button is on every page. If you select it, anything you have entered is cleared and you go to the BBC Weather website.

Pressing the Shift key 3 times works the same way.

Your browsing history will still show this website.

Use private browsing

Private browsing stops your browser saving your history. Turn on:

  • Incognito in Google Chrome
  • Private Window in Safari
  • Private Browsing in Mozilla Firefox
  • Secret mode in Samsung Internet
