Compare text readers

Check a letter reads photos with a text reader (OCR). This page runs several readers on the same photo so you can compare how well and how fast they read it.

Every reader runs on your device. Your photo is not sent anywhere. Most readers download their code and models from jsDelivr, Hugging Face, esm.sh or Baidu the first time they run – those sites see that a download happened, but not your photo.
