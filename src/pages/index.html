Get evidence of domestic abuse for legal aid

Find out which evidence you may already have, what each letter needs to say, and check a letter before you give it to your solicitor.

You might be able to get legal aid for a family matter, such as arrangements for your children, if you or your children have experienced domestic abuse and you cannot afford legal costs.

Letters are often returned by the Legal Aid Agency because of one or two words – for example "might be consistent" instead of "consistent". This service helps you ask for the right wording first time.

Before you start

You will not be asked to describe what happened to you. Nothing you enter is saved. Only questions you ask the helper are sent to an AI service to answer them.

! Warning If you are in immediate danger, call 999. If you cannot speak, call 999 and press 55 when prompted.

If you are at risk of harm, you can be fast-tracked to Civil Legal Advice: call 0345 345 4 345 and say you are at risk of harm, or use Check if you can get legal aid.

If you have been asked to write a letter

Doctors, support workers, refuges, councils and other professionals can write an evidence letter with the right wording. Solicitors can check several letters at once.
