Get help preparing evidence for legal aid for domestic abuse

If you have experienced domestic abuse, you might be able to get legal aid for a family case, such as arrangements for your children. You will usually need evidence of the abuse, such as a letter from a doctor, the police or a support service.

Use this service to:

  • find out what evidence you could get, and who to ask
  • ask for it – we write the request, and can send it for you
  • check a letter you have been given, before you give it to your solicitor

You will not be asked what happened to you, and your answers are not saved.

This service is for England and Wales. In Scotland, read legal aid on mygov.scot. In Northern Ireland, read legal aid on nidirect.

Start now

If you already have a letter

Check a letter you have been given to see if it has what the Legal Aid Agency needs, before you give it to your solicitor. You can take a photo of it, upload it or paste the text. The check happens on your device.

If you do not have a solicitor yet

You do not need evidence before you talk to a legal aid solicitor – they can tell you what they need. Find a legal aid adviser on GOV.UK and choose Family.
