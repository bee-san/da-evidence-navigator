Find a legal aid solicitor

If you have experienced domestic abuse, you might be able to get legal aid for a family case, such as arrangements for your children. A legal aid solicitor can help.

Answer 2 questions to find out how to get a solicitor. You will not be asked what happened to you, and your answers are not saved.
