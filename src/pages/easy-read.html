Getting help to pay for a lawyer

This page is in easy read. It uses short sentences.

If you are in danger now

Call 999.

If you cannot talk, call 999 and press 55.

What legal aid is

Legal aid means the government pays for your lawyer.

You might get legal aid for things like arrangements for your children.

You might get it if someone in your family has hurt you or controlled you.

What you need

You need a letter from someone who knows about the abuse.

This could be:

  • your doctor or nurse
  • a support worker
  • a refuge
  • your council
  • social services

The words in the letter matter

The letter must be sure.

"It is consistent with abuse" is good.

"It might be consistent with abuse" is not good enough.

How this website helps

  • It tells you who can write a letter.
  • It gives you a note to show them. The note has the right words.
  • It checks the letter when you get it.

You do not have to say what happened to you.

We do not keep anything you type.

Who can help

National Domestic Abuse Helpline: 0808 2000 247. It is free. You can call any time.

Civil Legal Advice: 0345 345 4 345. Say you are at risk of harm.
